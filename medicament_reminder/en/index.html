Last updated: 2026-11-25

1. Overview

medicament_reminder ("the app") is published by INFINITY GALAXY. This policy describes what the app does with information.

2. Data we collect

What you enter in the app is stored only on your device and is erased when you uninstall the app.

  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE

  • USE_EXACT_ALARM — Exact alarms, so reminders ring on time
