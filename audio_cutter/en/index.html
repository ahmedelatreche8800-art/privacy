Privacy Policy — Trimlo

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Overview

Trimlo ("the app"), published by INFINITY GALAXY, cuts a section out of an audio file. Everything happens on your device. The app has no server, no account and no internet access.

2. No data collection

We collect no personal data. No account or sign-up exists. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. It declares no internet permission and makes no network call.

3. Audio processing stays on your device

Decoding, the waveform, the preview, fades and the export are all done by your phone's own audio codecs (Android MediaCodec). No audio, file name or waveform ever leaves the device.

4. The files you choose

You pick a file through the Android system file picker. The app only receives the file you selected. It copies that file into its private cache so it can cut it. The copy is erased when you open another file, and all app data is erased when you uninstall the app. The clips you export are kept in the app's private folder, under "My cuts", until you delete them; nobody else can access them.

5. Sharing and saving (only when you ask)

"Share" hands the exported clip to your phone's share sheet, and only the app you choose receives it. "Save" opens the system "Save as" screen, and the file goes only where you decide. The app never sends anything by itself.

6. Ringtone

"Set as ringtone" copies the clip into the folder of the chosen sound (Ringtones, Notifications or Alarms) and makes it the matching default: call ringtone, notification sound or alarm. For "A contact's ringtone", you pick the person in the system contact list; the app changes only that contact's ringtone. It does not read, copy or send any other information from your contacts.

7. Permissions

Each permission is asked only when you choose the action that needs it, after an explanation in the app. If you refuse, that action is simply not done: nothing else changes and the app does not ask again on its own.

  • Modify system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): only to change the default call ringtone, notification sound or alarm.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS and WRITE_CONTACTS): only if you choose "A contact's ringtone", to give the clip to the contact you picked. No contact is read, stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and older only): used only to place the sound file on those versions.
  • No other permission: no internet, location, camera or microphone, and none to read your media library. Files are opened and saved through the system pickers, which need no permission.

8. Settings stored on your device

The language you choose in Settings is saved in a small file inside the app's private folder. It is never sent anywhere.

9. Third-party components

The app is built with Flutter. Local playback uses ExoPlayer (AndroidX Media3). Sharing uses the system share sheet (share_plus). None of these components sends data from the app.

10. Children

Trimlo is a general-audience tool. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

11. Changes to this policy

Any change will be published at this same address, with a new "last updated" date.
